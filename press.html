Description

ChickBoom: Multiverse Invaders is a hand-drawn 2D action shooter about holding the line with a rocket launcher. Invaders are pouring in from across the multiverse: barrel-powered flying war machines, ninja snails, a feathered army led by General Falcon, and modern jets, drones and tanks.

Survive each round, earn gold, and spend it in the store between fights. Combine simple items into powerful gear with unique effects, choose augments that change how you play, and charge up a special ability for when the screen gets crowded. Every world ends with a giant boss.

Features

  • 4 worlds, 20 levels: War Machines, Snails, Birds and Modern War.
  • 24 enemy types and 4 bosses: the Flying War Machine, the Snails King, General Falcon and the Phantom Fighter.
  • 32 items: 5 basic items combine into 9 advanced items and 17 unique items, each with a power of its own.
  • 21 augments, from Wildfire and Chain Reaction to Heat Seeker and the Mystery Egg.
  • A charged special ability that fills up as you fight.
  • Built for touch and mouse: virtual sticks on mobile, mouse aim on PC.
