Description

ChickBoom: Multiverse Invaders is a hand-drawn 2D action shooter starring one very unlucky chicken with a rocket launcher. Invaders are pouring in from across the multiverse: barrel-powered flying war machines, ninja snails, a feathered army led by General Falcon, and modern jets, drones and tanks.

Survive each round, earn gold, and spend it in the store between fights. Combine simple items into powerful gear with unique effects, choose augments that change how you play, and charge up a special ability that unleashes homing rockets. Every world ends with a giant boss.

Features

  • 4 worlds, 20 levels: War Machines, Snails, Birds and Modern War.
  • 24 enemy types and 4 bosses: the Flying War Machine, the Snails King, General Falcon and the Phantom Fighter.
  • 29 items to buy and combine into stronger gear with unique powers.
  • 13 augments, from Wildfire and Electric Shock to Orbital Strike and Guardian Arcs.
  • A charged special ability that fires homing rockets that never miss.
  • Built for touch and mouse: virtual sticks on mobile, mouse aim on PC.
