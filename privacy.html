Privacy Policy

ChickBoom: Multiverse Invaders

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play ChickBoom: Multiverse Invaders ("the game"), made by ScapePath Games ("we", "us"), on Android or Windows. In short: we do not collect your personal information.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no online multiplayer. We do not collect or store:

  • your name, email address, phone number or contacts;
  • your location;
  • photos, files or anything else on your device;
  • advertising IDs. The game contains no ads and no in-app purchases.

Game progress saved on your device

Your progress (completed levels, settings such as volume and aim sensitivity, and tutorial progress) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Technical diagnostics from the game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may automatically collect limited, anonymous technical data about the device and how the engine runs, such as device model, operating system, graphics hardware and performance or crash information. This data is used to keep the engine working well and does not identify you personally. It is handled under Unity's privacy policy.

App stores

If you download the game from Google Play or Steam, those stores handle your account, payment and download information under their own privacy policies: Google and Valve (Steam). We do not receive your personal information from them, only anonymous sales and install statistics.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no personal information is collected, none is shared or sold.

This website

scapepathgames.com does not use cookies, analytics or trackers. Like any website, its hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security for a short time.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if online features are added, we will update this page and the date at the top before that change goes live.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hello@scapepathgames.com.
