ChickBoom

Multiverse Invaders

One chicken. One RPG. Invaders pouring in from across the multiverse. Blast flying war machines, ninja snails, a feathered army and modern jets, build your arsenal between rounds, and take down giant bosses.

Coming soon onGoogle Play Coming soon onSteam (Windows)

Survive the invasion

ChickBoom is a 2D action shooter about one very unlucky chicken defending its home from wave after wave of invaders. Aim, dodge, jump, earn gold, and turn a simple rocket launcher into a weapon of legend.

Build your arsenal

Spend gold between rounds on 29 items, then combine them into powerful gear.

Unique item powers

Gale waves that blast enemies apart, echo shots, life-stealing blades and more.

Pick your augments

Choose game-changing augments like Wildfire, Electric Shock or Orbital Strike.

Four worlds of enemies

War Machines, Snails, Birds and Modern War, each with its own enemies and tricks.

Giant bosses

Face the Flying War Machine, the Snails King, General Falcon and the Phantom Fighter.

Unleash your special

Charge your special ability and fire a volley of homing rockets that never miss.

About ScapePath Games

ScapePath Games is an independent game studio making colourful, hand-drawn action games that are easy to pick up and hard to put down.

ChickBoom is our first game: built with love, a lot of rocket fuel and far too many chickens. It's coming to Android on Google Play and to Windows on Steam.
