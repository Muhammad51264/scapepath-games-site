Our first game

ChickBoom

Multiverse Invaders

Invaders are pouring in from across the multiverse. Grab a rocket launcher and hold the line: blast flying war machines, ninja snails, a feathered army and modern jets, build your arsenal between rounds, and take down giant bosses.

Coming soon onGoogle Play Coming soon onSteam (Windows)

Survive the invasion

ChickBoom is a 2D action shooter about defending the farm from wave after wave of invaders. Aim, dodge, jump, earn gold, and turn a simple rocket launcher into a weapon of legend.

Build your arsenal

Spend gold between rounds on 32 items, then combine them into powerful gear.

Unique item powers

Gale waves that blast enemies apart, boss-crushing hammers, life-stealing blades and more.

Pick your augments

Choose from 21 game-changing augments like Chain Reaction, Heat Seeker or the Mystery Egg.

Four worlds of enemies

War Machines, Snails, Birds and Modern War, each with its own enemies and tricks.

Giant bosses

Face the Flying War Machine, the Snails King, General Falcon and the Phantom Fighter.

Unleash your special

Fill up your special ability by fighting, then unleash it when the screen gets crowded.

Augments

Every few rounds you pick 1 of 3 augments, and it lasts the whole level. There are 21 to discover.

Health Over Hurt

Health
  • Double your health.
  • Deal 30% less damage.

Another Chance

Health
  • Revive once after being defeated.
  • Come back with 50% health.

Vampiric Rage

Health
  • Heal 10% of the damage you deal.
  • Take 5% more damage.

Feast

Health
  • Defeating an enemy heals 3% of your health.
  • Great against swarms of small enemies.

Big Bird

Health
  • Gain 30% more health.
  • Grow 25% bigger.
  • Move 15% slower.

Fresh Start

Health
  • Heal 25% of your health at the start of each round.

Critical Care

Health
  • Your heals can critically strike.
  • Same chance as your crit chance.
  • Critical heals heal double.

Deadly Aim

Power
  • Deal more damage the farther the enemy is.

Accumulating Might

Power
  • Each stack increases your damage by 2%.
  • Gain 1 stack on hit.
  • Lose all stacks on miss.
  • Max 10 stacks (+20% damage).

Volley Fire

Power
  • Fire 2 extra shots in two more directions.
  • Extra shots deal only 20% damage.

Wildfire

Power
  • Hits set enemies on fire for 10s.
  • Burning enemies take 2% of your damage every second.
  • Each hit lobs 2 fireballs to the sides that set enemies on fire.

Piercing Shot

Power
  • Shots pass through the first enemy and hit one more.
  • The second hit deals 50% damage.

Chain Reaction

Power
  • Defeated enemies explode.
  • The blast deals 25% damage to nearby enemies.
  • Enemies it defeats explode too.

Heat Seeker

Power
  • Rockets home in on the enemy you aim at.
  • Gain 10% cooldown reduction.
  • Deal 40% less damage.

Packrat

Special · later levels
  • Gain 1 extra item slot.

Electric Shock

Special
  • On hit, lightning deals 10% damage to all nearby enemies.
  • Stuns them for 0.3s.
  • With no one nearby, it strikes the enemy you hit.

Orbital Strike

Special
  • Every 3 hits, a satellite beams the enemy with the most health for 200%.
  • Extra shots don't count.

Guardian Arcs

Special
  • 3 rotating arcs block attacks.
  • An arc breaks when hit.
  • Broken arcs return after 20s.

Iron Reflexes

Special · later levels
  • Gain 5% dodge chance per 10 armor.
  • Up to 20% dodge chance.

Overcharge

Special
  • Your special charges 35% faster.

Mystery Egg

Special · later levels
  • Hatch 2 random augments.
  • Also get a random drawback.
